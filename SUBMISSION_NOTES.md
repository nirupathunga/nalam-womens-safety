# NALAM — Submission checklist

## Pitch
NALAM connects privacy exposure checks, incident documentation, evidence integrity indicators and next-step guidance in one survivor-controlled mobile workflow.

## 90-second demo
1. Show Privacy Check using fictional text such as `Contact demo@example.com or 9876543210`.
2. Add a fictional incident.
3. Open the timeline and incident detail to show its SHA-256 fingerprint.
4. Export the PDF report.
5. Show English/Tamil toggle and support screen.
6. Explain limitations honestly: no live monitoring or emergency dispatch; hashes are integrity indicators, not proof of truth.

## Suggested deck
1. Title and one-line value proposition.
2. Problem: prevention, documentation and support are fragmented.
3. Solution: one user-controlled workflow.
4. Demo walkthrough.
5. Technical design: Expo, local storage, pattern matching, SHA-256, PDF export.
6. Privacy and safeguards.
7. Limitations and roadmap.
8. Impact measurement: task completion and time to assemble a report in a controlled demo.

Do not claim the product is globally unique or production-secure without validation.
